Coverage and verified sources

Standard Luas network only, including Red Line branches and Green Line one-way stops. Planned journeys are indicative network routes, not live service guarantees. No journey times or walking distances are estimated. Check current service messages before travelling. Live departures are snapshots, timestamped separately; use their refresh button to update.

Supported places include Lucan (suggested access via Red Cow Park & Ride), Eden Quay, the Spire, O'Connell Street, Trinity College, IFSC, both NCI campuses, Dundrum Town Centre, Tallaght Hospital, Sandyford Business District, Heuston and Connolly stations. NCI and broad street/business-district names prompt clarification. Access stops are curated suggestions, not a GPS-based nearest-stop calculation; exact addresses and mobility needs can change the best choice.

About myLUAS

Live Luas forecasts supplied by Transport Infrastructure Ireland through the existing Luas data service. Arrivals refresh every 20 seconds; each stop shows its last successful update. Times and service messages come from the data feed.

Your selected stop and up to two favourites are stored in this browser. Walking estimates use your current GPS position only when requested and are not route guidance or a guarantee of catching a tram.
